About Us

Homestyle Filipino food, cooked every day

Tita Maricar's is a neighbourhood carinderia in Calo, Bay, Laguna — a panciteria and lutong-ulam kitchen serving fresh daily ulam and made-to-order trays for the people nearby, students, and families celebrating something special.

We cook in small batches with good ingredients, so the menu changes with the day. Come hungry, or message us first to check what's ready.

— Salamat, and see you po!
